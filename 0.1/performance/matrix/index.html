    Every combination¶

    The switches (result cache, parallel quotes, follow-up delivery and the database layout) were run in every combination, in every mode: 88 runs, each with 16 clients, 1,500 measured orders, a 200-order warm-up and a VACUUM ANALYZE, one run per cell. Databases are shared (one PostgreSQL instance with a schema per module) or split (every deployable unit on its own instance: three for the groups, eight for the services, so the one-process modes have no split run, and split-dbs is always split). A dash means the combination does not exist for that mode.

    Because every cell is a single run, differences of about 5% are noise. The other pages use the cells of this table that vary one switch at a time (cache and parallel quotes, follow-up delivery, the database layout).

    What the combinations show¶

    • The mode decides, the switches adjust. In every combination the order of the modes is the same: modulith, macro-modulith and loopback-codec together at the top, then loopback-http, then groups and split-dbs, then the two eight-service modes at the bottom. No setting of any switch changes that.
    • The effects roughly multiply. In microservices the cache is worth +18% and parallel quotes +15%; with both off the throughput is 116 orders/s against 161 with both on, +39%, which is about the product of the two. Switching events on costs a further 3 to 7%, so the worst combination is 112 orders/s against 161 for the best (30% lower) in microservices, against 387 and 450 (14% lower) in modulith.
    • The switches matter most where calls are most expensive. The worst combination is 14 to 16% below the best in the one-process modes, 22% below in loopback-http, 13% below in groups and 30 to 31% below with eight services.
    • Outbox against events is a small cost. Events were behind the outbox in 40 of the 44 pairs of cells, by 4% at the median, within a range of 7% behind to 5% ahead.
    • Splitting the databases changes nothing measurable. Shared against split is within noise in all three modes that have the choice (the comparison), and in every combination of the other switches.

    The raw reports of the 88 runs are in the raw reports.
